BLOCKCRAFT V6.30 - THE ONE-MINUTE IN-GAME CINEMATIC TRAILER
===========================================================

HOW TO WATCH
  * Title screen: press "Watch Trailer (1:00)" (the bottom button).
  * While playing: type /trailer in chat.
  * It runs for exactly 60 seconds and then returns automatically.
  * ANY KEY or a mouse click skips it immediately.
  * Everything is restored afterwards: your screen, camera position,
    view mode, daylight, weather, colour grading and options.

THIS IS NOT A VIDEO
  No movie file is shipped. The trailer is eight scripted camera moves
  through a real, freshly generated world (fixed seed 20261108), drawn
  by the game's own renderer with the same chunks, trees, caves, sky,
  weather and shaders as normal play. It has 2.39:1 letterboxing, hard
  cuts, title cards, a dawn-to-night-to-dawn time-lapse, a forced
  thunderstorm, a four-act soundtrack and foley stings from the real
  CC0 WAV recordings in sounds/foley/. The logo is built from the
  game's own block textures.
