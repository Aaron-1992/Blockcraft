BLOCKCRAFT V6.15 - NATIVE CREATE PROJECT: VISIBLE MACHINERY / BELTS
================================================================
This is still an INCOMPLETE native Create-inspired implementation, NOT the
full official mod or a Forge loader. No Minecraft install required.

CHANGED IN V6.15
---------------
- Eight original 32px machine-component surfaces, not stretched planks/stone
  block textures. Face UVs are cropped at consistent texel density. All
  machine models (including held/Creative views) use these new components.
- Shafts/gears/wheels, press ram and belt treads render every frame with
  partial-tick interpolation. They no longer remesh chunks at 10Hz to rotate.
  Smoothness still depends on your frame rate; software CI is not a GPU FPS
  guarantee. Rendered machinery range is currently 96 blocks.
- Straight horizontal powered belts and depots hold visible, persistent
  stacks. Dropped items are captured. Belt direction follows signed RPM.
  Cargo stops with power, waits for occupied receiving belts, and falls off
  open ends. Depots are passive. One stack per belt segment/depot for now.
- Presses stamp complete visible batches on a belt or depot TWO blocks below
  the press block. The belt pauses the batch under the ram until stamped.
  There is no press inventory screen in normal gameplay.
- Millstone: drop ingredients into it, or use it with ingredients in hand.
  Empty-hand use collects the output (or input if there is no output).
  It no longer opens a machine inventory screen.
- Ordinary motor/wrench/wheel interactions no longer print status in chat.
  RPM/faults and short interaction hints appear only when aiming at parts.
- Water wheels derive signed torque from ACTUAL water-current vectors:
  falling water on opposite sides turns opposite ways; horizontal gradients
  also drive them. Still water gives no torque; opposing currents cancel.
  Right-click cannot manually reverse water any more. Horizontal axle only.

QUICK START
-----------
Back up your saves, including kinetics.dat. Use V6.15 on all peers; do not
open a new belt/depot save in V6.14 (the older reader does not know those kinds).

Search "create" or "kinetic" in Creative. /kinetics builds a clear-air
ordinary-block workshop with a powered belt, overhead press, receiving depot,
gear/redstone branch and a glass-sided waterfall driving a wheel. Watch eight
ingots travel under the press, become sheets, and arrive on the depot. The
command refuses occupied/unloaded space; it doesn't clear existing builds.

Belts are placed SEGMENT BY SEGMENT in a straight horizontal line in this
version, not with Create's two-pulley connector tool. Floor placement aligns
with your facing. Use the Kinetic Wrench to switch the two horizontal axes.
Connect a motor or shaft to a belt's axle side; adjacent collinear segments
share power. For an X axle the belt travels along Z; for a Z axle along X.
Positive/negative motor speeds reverse travel. Vertical/inclined/turning belts,
funnels, tunnels, filtering, bulk stacking and actor transport are unfinished.

Right-click a belt/depot with an item: place one. Sneak-use: place the held
stack. Empty hand: collect its visible stack. You can instead drop items onto
the surface (ordinary item dropping). Stacks and belt positions persist in
kinetics.dat; breaking a belt/depot returns its cargo once.

Place a press at (x,y+2,z), with a belt/depot at (x,y,z). Connect power to the
press axle. Iron ingots -> iron sheets; gold ingots -> golden sheets. One
batch operation takes 6400 accumulated absolute RPM-ticks (100 ticks at 64
RPM, 50 at 128). Press and belt can have independent drive speeds. For a
millstone: wheat -> flour, cobble -> gravel, gravel -> sand; flour smelts to
bread. Millstone output collection is manual in this update; funnels aren't
implemented. Old V6.14 press inventories/progress are preserved and can finish
processing; empty-hand use collects them. No new press input goes into that
legacy storage, and visible pressing waits until old input is cleared.

Mechanical controls remain: use Creative Motor to cycle speed (sneak reverses
cycling); use wrench to rotate parts. Shafts 1:1; adjacent small cogs reverse;
diagonal large/small cogs make reciprocal 2:1 gearing; perpendicular large
cogs provide bevel gearing. Powered clutch disconnects, powered gearshift
reverses. Gauges show signed speed/stress through the target readout.

LIMITS / HONEST SCOPE
--------------------
The wheel still has compact native geometry, NOT the complete Create
multiblock wheel/obstruction rules. It makes signed 8 RPM when there is net
rim torque. Capacity 32 SU/RPM; Creative Motor 16384 SU/RPM. Press costs
8 SU/RPM, millstone 4. Native maximum 256 RPM, 4096 loaded kinetic blocks,
16384 saved machine/cargo inventories. Unloaded network frontiers, conflicts,
overspeed and overstress stop the network rather than destroying blocks.

Controls/cargo processing remain host-only. Full multiplayer synchronization,
contraptions, trains, logistics, fluid pipes/boilers, most processing, complete
survival recipes, schematics and tutorials remain unfinished. Models, collision
shapes and recipes are still native approximations, not a full parity claim.
The guide/roadmap in docs/create/STATUS.md tracks the remaining work.
