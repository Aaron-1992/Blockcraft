# V6.21 — the caves were completely rebuilt, and weather now reaches the horizon

The V6.19 "bigger caves" were explicitly rejected: deep worlds had become one
continuous swiss-cheese cavern. V6.21 replaces that generator outright for new
worlds and adds real underground content. Weather was rebuilt for beauty near
**and far**, not just around the player. Old saves keep their exact old terrain.

## A new cave engine (`src/bc/CaveWorks.java`)

`TerraGen` now selects the generator by the saved `terrainVer`: 1 = classic,
2 = V6.19 (`carveBig`), **3 = V6.21** (`CaveWorks`). `CaveWorks` is a pure
function of the seed and world coordinates, plus a cached seed-derived site
plan, so neighbouring chunks and worker threads always agree on a block.

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| Winding tunnels | A ridged 3D noise pair with a depth-dependent radius: calm near the surface, roomy at y=16–54, tighter again near bedrock — instead of "the deeper, the more hollow". |
| Sparse chambers | A high-threshold 3D noise gated by a slow 2D province, so rooms cluster into cave regions rather than merging into one hollow. |
| Giant halls | Rare hashed ellipsoids on a 96-block cell grid, only in the deep layer. |
| Lava provinces | Lava appears in designated deep provinces and lava springs/pools, not under every carved block. |
| Depth strata | Horizontal undulating four-block bands of granite / diorite / andesite / dark deep stone, exposed on cave walls. Bands, not random speckles. |
| Cave mouths | Short-scale noise with a strict threshold plus hashed sinkhole shafts: rare real openings, not a pockmarked surface. |
| "Stuff" | Stalactites, stalagmites, columns, rubble, ore pockets, crystals, glowcaps, springs, pools, vines, mushrooms and the odd spawner, scaled by Cave Richness. |

`hollow()` returns solid / air / lava per coordinate; `mouth()` and `shaft()`
place entrances; `finish()` applies strata, wall lining, lakes and decorations
and then recomputes the chunk heightmap. Compared with V6.19, the new field
hollows out **less than half** as much rock in the headless sample while still
producing connected caves (`Tests621.caveField`).

## Six underground site types

Sites are hashed onto a 112-block region grid: one candidate per region, ~40%
valid, deterministic, each with its own carved domed chamber, four corridors
reaching toward natural tunnels, a colonnade, beams, a pedestal with **a loot
chest**, lighting, details and supports.

| Type | Build |
|---|---|
| 0 ruined shrine | mossy / cracked stone brick, chiseled pedestal, vines |
| 1 crystal geode | quartz, purpur, sea lanterns and stained glass |
| 2 lava forge | obsidian, cobble, a lava pool and ceiling lava drips |
| 3 mineshaft hall | planks, logs, fences, torches and coal |
| 4 mushroom grotto | mycelium, podzol, mushroom stems and caps |
| 5 flooded cistern | prismarine, dark prismarine, water ring, lily pads |

`CaveWorks.siteY()` samples the real `Gen.column(x,z).h` once per cached plan
and pushes the site down when the local surface is low. Without this, a site in
a shallow ocean would sit **above** the sea floor, floating in the water
column — found by rendering a real generator cross-section
(`docs/screenshots/v6.21-caves.png`). Underground structures are gated by
`st.caveStructures`, **not** by the surface `st.structures` switch, so turning
surface structures off still leaves the caves populated.

## Weather near and far (`WeatherFx`, `Sky`, `ShaderSrc`, `Shaders`)

* **Distant curtains.** The old two rings (inner/outer) became a
  reach-scaled grid of rain/snow curtain cells from the player out toward the
  horizon. Weather Detail builds 1 / 2 / 3 crossing planes per cell; Weather
  Distance scales the outer ring 0.5×–1.5×. Curtains fade with distance,
  tint for storm, snow and low sun, brighten near the storm core, and light up
  around a lightning bolt when Lightning Glow is on.
* **Wind is real.** `windShear()` derives curtain lean and texture scroll from
  `Weather.Snap.windX/windZ`; Wind Strength scales it 0.2×–2.0×.
* **Ground haze.** A generated soft blob texture (`groundHaze`) is uploaded to
  `texMist` and drawn as drifting low quads under distant rain (`nMist`),
  giving storms a visible base on the horizon.
* **Rainbows.** `rainbowStrengthNow()` needs wet ground, clearing rain, a
  visible low sun and the Rainbows option. `Sky.draw` calls
  `WeatherFx.drawRainbow(SUN, bow)` after the dome: seven coloured bands built
  from `rainbowDir` around the antisolar point.
* **Clouds.** `CLOUD_FS` gained `uniform float wxBeauty` (set from
  `Options.weatherQuality` in `Shaders.renderClouds`), a cold ice halo, a
  stronger silver lining and a warm sunset rim that adds `sunCol * rim`.
* All settings are in **Options → Video → Debug Video → Advanced** under
  `WEATHER & SKY`, persist in `options.txt` and are clamped on load.

## Settings

`WorldSettings` gained `caveStructures`, `caveLayers`, `caveLakes`,
`caveRichness` and `terrainVer=3`; `WorldPreview`'s cache key includes them,
so the Create New World preview is regenerated when they change.
`Options` gained `caveStructures`, `caveLayers`, `caveLakes`, `caveRichness`,
`weatherQuality`, `weatherRainbow`, `weatherMist`, `weatherBoltGlow`,
`weatherWind` and `weatherReach`. `Screens.ADV_ROWS` is **57**: new
`V6.21 CAVES` and `WEATHER & SKY` sections plus the V6.20 maps section, and
`Reset Advanced` restores the new defaults.

| Setting | Default | Range |
|---|---|---|
| V6.21 Caves (new worlds) | on | on/off |
| Cave Structures / Strata / Lakes | on | on/off each |
| Cave Richness | 1.00× | 0.40–2.00 |
| Weather Detail | Detailed | Plain / Normal / Detailed |
| Weather Distance | 1.00× | 0.50–1.50 |
| Wind Strength | 1.00× | 0.20–2.00 |
| Ground Mist / Rainbows / Lightning Glow | on | on/off each |

  V6.21 regression groups (`test/bc/Tests621.java`) for option persistence and
  clamping, the Advanced rows, new-world settings, the calmer coordinate-pure
  cave field, surface-aware sites and horizontal strata, deterministic
  structures, generated chunks with caves/strata/loot, distant weather colour
  helpers, quality-dependent curtain and mist meshes, and shader/version wiring.
  `Tests619`/`Tests620` were updated for `V6.21 Caves`, `terrainVer=3` and
  `ADV_ROWS=57`; `Tests38` keeps using the restored legacy `WeatherFx.build`
  overload.
* Strict equivalence: terrain / world / tick fingerprints are still identical
  to the original Update 3.5 jar; **397 fingerprints identical**, 64 changed
  on purpose, 150 new.
* `tools/glsl_check.py`: all **21** shaders parse, including the modified
  `CLOUD_FS` and `CLOUD_SHADOW_FS`.
* `python3 tools/build.py` → `[build] OK (Update 6.21)`, 164 sources,
  `build/blockcraft.jar` 7.29 MB / 1,225 entries, `build/Blockcraft.exe`
  7.36 MB. `--patch` produced
  `releases/Blockcraft-UPDATE6.21-patch.zip` (39,038,991 bytes).
* `tools/verify_release.py` (via `tools/make_release.py 6.21`): both packaged
  launchers report `version=6.21`, 32px atlas, pixel-sprite bow;
  `source.sha256=aaed16b8ab10d6353af8908c195106acb2c6d721113994d157852cf675965726`.
  The build receipt and verifier now also record/check the hashes of
  `CaveWorks`, `WeatherFx`, `TerraGen`, `Sky`, `WorldSettings` and

* `docs/screenshots/v6.21-caves.png` is a real generator cross-section (V6.19

Real-GPU / Xvfb shader compilation, GPU screenshots, measured frame-time
