# V6.23 — more tree variants and bigger trees for every species

This update adds a **variant + size catalogue** to the new-world tree generator. It is
not one new tree: every species that the world can grow now has several distinct
silhouettes and three size tiers, and two new in-game knobs control how big they get.
Old saves keep their exact old terrain; all changes apply to newly created worlds.

## The catalogue (`src/bc/TerraTrees.java`)

Eight species, **34 silhouettes** in total, each available at Normal / Large / Giant:

| Species | Variants | Silhouettes |
|---|---|---|
| Oak | 6 | classic lobed, small squat, tall columnar, wide umbrella, forked twin trunk, weeping with hanging tips |
| Birch | 5 | classic narrow, tall pale, three-stem clump, weeping, two-tier grand |
| Spruce | 6 | tiered, sparse pine, columnar, 2×2 giant spruce, 2×2 grand spruce, candle spire |
| Jungle | 5 | small, tall layered, 2×2 giant, huge flat canopy with buttress roots, very tall emergent |
| Acacia | 4 | bent umbrella, tall double-bend, wide plate, forked two-arm |
| Dark oak | 3 | classic, grand layered, wide spreading |
| Swamp | 3 | wide oak with vines, swamp cypress with knees, weeping willow |
| Bush | 2 | round bush, tall bush |

Giant mushrooms and ice spikes also gained the same size tiers, so no large plant is
left at a fixed size.

For each tree `TerraGen` derives a **variant** and a **size tier** from the same
seed-derived hash it already used for the shape (`ts`), so a tree crossing a chunk
border is rebuilt identically by every neighbouring chunk. The biome's original plan
(e.g. "tall birch", "mega spruce") biases the choice instead of being discarded, but
all variants of that species really appear: roughly 58% Normal, 30% Large, 12% Giant.

Giant conifers grow **tall, not fat**: their tiers are spaced three blocks apart, the
interior is hollowed out more aggressively and their crowns stay narrow. That is why
adding bigger trees did not undo the V6.22 taiga fix (see below).

## Safety: bigger without the lag spike

- `TerraTrees.Buf` grew only from 29×60×29 to 31×64×31 cells (~0.49 MB per generator
  thread) and has a **hard budget of 900 leaf blocks per tree**. No silhouette/size
  combination can exceed it, even at the maximum Tree Size.
- The V6.22 per-chunk tree cap and `treeDensity` multiplier are unchanged.
- Headless 3×3 taiga meshing at seed 6120, bushy leaves on: V6.22 = 297 spruce leaves /
  165,648 vertices / 36.2 ms; **V6.23 = 229 leaves / 162,548 vertices / 34.3 ms**.
  More variety and bigger trees, slightly *less* work in the biome that used to be
  the game's bottleneck. These are headless meshing timings, not FPS claims.

## New settings

| Setting | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| **Tree Size** (0.70×–1.50×) | Options → Video → Debug Video → Advanced | Multiplies the height and crown of **every** species on every tier. Copied into the world at creation. |
| **Giant Trees** (on/off) | same row | Off removes the Giant tier (it becomes Large) — cheaper on slower machines. Copied into the world at creation. |

Both persist in `options.txt`, are stored in the world save (`WorldSettings`), are
clamped on load, and are part of the Create New World preview cache key. Old saves
default to 1.0× / on, so their existing trees are untouched. `ADV_ROWS` grows 60 → 61.

`docs/screenshots/v6.23-tree-variants.png` shows a front elevation of every
(species, variant) at the Large tier; `v6.23-tree-sizes.png` shows one silhouette per
species across Normal / Large / Giant. Both are headless renders of the **real**
`TerraTrees.Buf` geometry, not GPU screenshots.
