# V6.38 — Classic UI, seasons and local climate

V6.38 restores the classic `Screens.java` route for the title, worlds, world creation, options and pause flow. Spacing and labels are kept readable, and the live Create New World map preview and numeric settings remain. This rolls back the later rejected UI redraws; it does not claim those menu designs were accepted.

## Seasons

- A deterministic four-season cycle follows the world's saved day clock; seasons can be enabled or disabled and their length is adjustable.
- Seasonal temperature, precipitation type, crop growth, grass and foliage tint respond to the cycle.
- `/season next`, `/season on`, and `/season off` provide quick controls in the Overworld.

## Player temperature and wind

- An optional HUD reports season, ambient air temperature, feels-like temperature and wind.
- Player climate considers biome, altitude, time of day, shelter, rain exposure, water, nearby heat and local wind chill.
- Ordinary breezes no longer push the player; only exposed players in extreme gusts above 18 m/s get a small movement nudge.
- Animated wind streaks remain visible in dry weather, and shader foliage/plant sway follows the local wind and gusts.
- Severe exposure has a small capped movement/exhaustion effect; it is not a health-damage survival overhaul.
- A deterministic, spatially varying wind field responds to the world seed, time, location, altitude and world wind strength. Wind affects weather drift, exposed player movement and feels-like temperature.

## Island world types

Create New World offers Small Islands (256 chunks wide), Medium Islands (512 chunks wide), and Big Islands (1028 chunks wide). Each creates a finite, irregular, biome-rich island with beaches and open ocean beyond its coast. Chunk counts use 16x16-block chunks; the regular terrain, cave, plant and structure systems remain active.

## Preserved V6.37 work

The V6.37 deep-world and rendering changes remain the base: lifted cloud/weather heights and rendering/culling across 24 vertical sections. Climate code is layered onto that history rather than replacing it. No Mojang code or assets are included.

## Verification limits

The headless unit suite, equivalence check, GLSL validation, Windows package build and release verification are run as available. This environment has no real GPU/Windows playtest, so none is claimed.
