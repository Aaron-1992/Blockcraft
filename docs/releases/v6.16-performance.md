# V6.16 — PC chunk/performance test build

## Scope

This release is based on V6.15 and keeps its gameplay, textures, machinery, world format, and normal graphics settings. It targets the PC chunk-load path; render distance and visual quality were not reduced.

- Chunk generation remains asynchronous. A dedicated low-priority lighting worker now handles lighting for newly integrated chunks, with a bounded backlog of eight jobs. When full, ready chunks are requeued instead of blocking the update thread.
- A per-world lock coordinates lighting with block edits, synchronous chunk loads, and world resets. Chunks are withheld from meshing while their lighting pass is active.
- Chunk eviction scans at most 128 loaded chunks and removes at most two per update. Overworld LOD refresh scans at most 512 chunks and refreshes at most four summaries per tick.
- Per-section non-air counts make empty-section and empty-chunk checks constant-time once occupancy has been indexed. Counts are rebuilt after bulk generation/loading and updated by ordinary block writes.
- Mesher and LOD workers run below normal thread priority; LOD worker concurrency is capped at two.
