# V6.30 — a one-minute cinematic trailer, in game

The user asked: *"Make a cinematic trailer ingame 1 minute long Please"*.

V6.30 adds a **real in-game trailer**, not a video file and not a
slideshow. It is eight scripted camera moves through a freshly generated
world, rendered by the game's own renderer with the same chunks, trees,
caves, sky, weather and shaders as normal play.

## How to watch

* **Watch Trailer (1:00)** — a new fifth row on the title screen.
* **`/trailer`** — works while playing, in any world.
* Runs for **exactly 60 seconds**, then returns automatically.
* **Any key or mouse click skips it.**
* On exit the game restores the screen, camera position, view mode,
  daylight, weather state, colour grading and options exactly as they
  were — including from inside a live world.

## What it is made of

`src/bc/Trailer.java` holds a deterministic shot table: eight 7.5-second
shots, seed `20261108`, fixed angles/radii/travel distances/heights. All
camera maths is pure and testable headlessly (`shotAt`, `skyAt`,
`cameraAt`, `plan`).

| # | Time | Card | Look |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0:00–0:07 | A WORLD OF YOUR OWN | dawn, 38→26 blocks up, 64-block glide |
| 2 | 0:07–0:15 | FORESTS, MOUNTAINS, OCEANS | low 16→11 block dolly through terrain |
| 3 | 0:15–0:22 | WEATHER WITH A HORIZON | forced thunderstorm, 6000→7600 dayTime |
| 4 | 0:22–0:30 | BENEATH THE SURFACE | very low flight over cave country |
| 5 | 0:30–0:37 | BUILD ANYTHING | climbing sunset shot |
| 6 | 0:37–0:45 | PLAY TOGETHER | fast dusk flyover |
| 7 | 0:45–0:52 | AND WHEN NIGHT FALLS | moonlight descent, 15200→18500 |
| 8 | 0:52–1:00 | BLOCKCRAFT | fade to black, the block-built logo |

Every shot:

* computes the camera height from the **real terrain column** so it never
  flies into the ground, and aims at a point 26 blocks ahead on the
  ground — the frame always contains real landscape;
* interpolates `world.dayTime` for a continuous one-minute time-lapse
  (the sun/moon, sky colours and lighting all follow);
* sets the sky grading to **Cinematic** and restores it on exit;
* plays one of the real CC0 WAV foley recordings on the cut
  (`wind_gust`, `leaf_rustle`, `cave_rumble`, `cave_wind`,
  `geode_chime`, `cave_drip`);
* selects one of four soundtrack acts from tracks already in the
  soundbank (`lce_menu_2`, `lce_battle_city_1`, `lce_battle_4`,
  `lce_credits`) via `SoundEngine.selectConsoleMusic`.

The overlay draws 2.39:1 letterbox bars, a thin gold rule, chapter and
timecode slate, a 60-second progress line, a skip hint, fade-in from
black, a 0.22 s hard-cut flash, title cards that fade in/out, and a final
fade into the game's own `Logo.draw()` block logo. No external video
decoder, no new binary assets, no Mojang assets.

`Trailer.start()` from the menus stops the turning panorama, installs a
throw-away world with the V6.29 generation (`terrainVer 3`, `caveVer 4`),
finds a dry, varied anchor deterministically, and disposes the world on
exit before restarting the panorama. From a live world it keeps that
world and only drives the player camera, so nothing is saved or changed.

`java -jar blockcraft.jar --trailer-plan` prints the whole shot list with
no window or GPU. See `docs/releases/v6.30-trailer-plan.txt`, the headless
storyboard `docs/screenshots/v6.30-trailer.png` and
`package/Trailer-guide.txt`.
