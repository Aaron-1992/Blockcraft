# V6.32 — the world is solid again: caves and structures rebuilt to Minecraft density

The user reported: *"buddy the world is just basically non existant because of the caves redo them exactly like minecraft same with the structures"*.

"Exactly like Minecraft" here means **Minecraft 1.18/1.20 generation style, density and
structure vocabulary, implemented originally**. Per the project's standing rule, no
Mojang code or assets are introduced: every carver, every shape and every structure
below is written from scratch with this game's own blocks.

## The actual problem, measured

A headless 96×96 land sample at seed 20261107 (both versions use the same terrain):

| | carved overall | carved within 20 blocks of the surface | surface-open columns |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V6.29 (`caveVer 4`) | 14.5% | 11.9% | 187 (2.0%) |
| V6.31 (`caveVer 5`) | 16.1% | **19.3%** | **930 (10.1%)** |
| **V6.32 (`caveVer 6`)** | **5.0%** | **1.2%** | **40 (0.43%)** |

V6.31 really did hollow out nearly a fifth of the ground right under the player's
feet and turn one column in ten into a hole. That is why the world looked
"basically non existent".

## New carver — `Caves6.java` (`caveVer = 6`, new worlds only)

* **An intact 12-block surface crust.** Nothing at all is carved in the top 12
  blocks except at a genuine cave entrance. This alone fixes the swiss-cheese
  surface.
* **Spaghetti / noodle tunnels**: thin ridged-noise worm pairs at two scales,
  most common between y=20 and y=60, tapering off towards daylight.
* **Cheese caves**: two sparse large 3D-noise fields clustered into slow 2D
  provinces, y=8..72, with a high gate so they are discrete rooms, not a void.
* **Rare giant halls** and **local aquifers** (water below a hashed level) and
  lava lakes only at the very bottom, like the 1.18 aquifer system.
* **Entrances**: a strict 2D aperture gate selects a tiny fraction of dry land;
  a funnel that widens downward runs 26 blocks into the ordinary cave network.
  `caveBelow()` is evaluated from the **surface height**, so the shaft is
  continuous for the whole column instead of a row of disconnected holes.
  Openings are pure noise outlines — craters, ragged slits, wide throats — and
  never a repeated template. Oceans, rivers and beaches stay sealed.
* `mouthSurface()` (tree and spawn exclusion) now asks the carver itself whether
  a cave reached daylight.

Headless verification: 40 surface openings in 6 separate components,
**40/40 connected** at least 10 blocks down into carved cave; 5.0% of the world
carved overall, 7.3% deep down, 1.2% near the surface. `docs/screenshots/v6.32-caves.png`
shows the cross-section and the almost fully intact top-down surface — a headless
generated preview, **not** a GPU screenshot.

## New structures — `CaveStructures6.java`

V6.31 built huge rotundas with side chambers and 20-block corridors, which
hollowed the world further. V6.32 replaces them with six small, recognisable
builds on a 128-block grid (all deterministic and clipped to chunk seams in
world coordinates, each with a short tunnel into a real cave):

| Type | What it is built from |
| --- | --- |
| **Mineshaft** | 3-wide plank-floored corridors, oak log supports, torches, iron track lines, a loot chest |
| **Dungeon** | small cobble/mossy room, spawner in the middle, two chests |
| **Stronghold** | stone-brick / chiselled / mossy rooms, a bookshelf library, an obsidian portal frame, chest |
| **Amethyst Geode** | spherical obsidian → purpur → amethyst-glass shell with a hollow crystal interior |
| **Ancient City** | dark-prismarine floor and low walls, sea lantern, chest |
| **Ruined Portal** | obsidian frame, cracked-stone-brick rubble, glowstone, chest |

Link tunnels never erase loot or spawners. All six types were verified to occur
and to place their characteristic blocks in generated chunks.

## What stays unchanged

V6.31 worlds (`caveVer = 5`), V6.29 worlds (`caveVer = 4`) and every older save
keep their exact old caves and structures — `Caves5`/`CaveStructures5` remain in
the source and are still used for them. LOD cross-fades, the 24-shot montage
trailer, borderless fullscreen, the clean menus, the real CC0 sounds and all
earlier work are untouched. The trailer's throw-away world now uses `caveVer = 6`.
