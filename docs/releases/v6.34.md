# V6.34 — caves completely redone, structures integrated into the cave system

The user asked: *"caves are still disgusting redo it kompletely the way minecraft does you are
just making holes from the top of the terrain that goes straight down for a bit until it hits a
cave also redo all the structures so that they actually work with the cave system"*.

V6.32's carver kept a 12-block crust solid, picked a rare 2D aperture and bored a wide funnel
straight down until it hit a cave. The headless before/after cross-section in
`docs/screenshots/v6.34-caves.png` shows exactly what was wrong: rectangular vertical shafts
sitting on top of otherwise unrelated caverns. V6.29's hashed bowls/ramps and V6.28's hashed
mouths had the same problem. **All of those entrance systems are gone.**

## One continuous 3D cave volume (`caveVer = 7`, new worlds only)

`src/bc/Caves7.java` is a new, original implementation of the Minecraft 1.18/1.20 *shape and
density* approach — no Mojang code, data or assets. There is **no entrance generator at all**.
Instead a single 3D field is evaluated in **absolute world Y** and carved from bedrock up to the
terrain surface:

| Layer | How it is made |
| --- | --- |
| Spaghetti / noodle tunnels | Two ridged 3D Perlin noise pairs. A tunnel follows the curve where both noises are near zero. Coordinates are **sheared in Y** (`x + 0.26y`, `z - 0.17y`) so tunnels lean and meander as they rise instead of standing vertically. |
| Long-scale noodles | A second, coarser ridged-noise pair at roughly half the frequency, giving long connected tunnels. |
| Cheese caverns | Two high-gate 3D noise fields clustered by a slow 2D "province" noise, strongest around y=44 and fading out above y=70. Large multi-room caverns, not one endless void. |
| Giant halls | Rare hashed ellipsoids on a 128-block grid, deep only (y=9..40). |
| Aquifers / lava | Local hashed water levels and lava lakes below y=10. |
| Vertical profile | A bell in **absolute Y** (centre y=44, sigma 30), not depth below the local surface. Mountains therefore stay solid and valleys / lowlands get mouths. |

`carve(x, y, z, surfaceH, allowSurface)` returns solid / air / lava / water. Its only special
case is a thin seal under **ocean** floors; on dry land the same field runs all the way to the
grass. **A cave mouth is simply the place where the field intersects the terrain.** The mouth's
walls are the cave's own sheared noise walls, so they are sloped, ragged and different every time.
There is no aperture noise, no funnel, no shaft, no drilled hole, no hashed bowl and no ramp.

`TerraGen` disables the old sinkhole `mouth()`, the `shaft()` column, the `CaveCliffs.Mouth`
objects and the stone entrance rim for `cc7`; ravine frequency is raised to a `need` of 0.60 so
surface cuts come from caves rather than top-down cracks. Trees and villages check
`CaveWorks.mouthSurface()`, which now resolves through `Caves7.surfaceOpen()`.

## Structures are part of the same air volume

`src/bc/CaveStructures7.java` replaces the V6.32 pattern of "sealed box + short link tunnel".
`CaveStructures7.air(x, y, z, surfaceH)` is called **from `Caves7.cause()` for every block**, so
mineshaft corridors, dungeon rooms, stronghold halls, geode interiors, ancient-city plazas and
ruined-portal areas are literally part of the one cave air field. A cheese cavern can cut straight
through a mineshaft; a worm tunnel can open into a dungeon; nothing is ever sealed or artificially
connected.

`finish(ch)` only stamps floors, walls, supports and loot into air the carver already made, and
only where the neighbouring rock is solid — so a cave passing through a wall stays open. Plans
are deterministic in `(seed, region)` and cached, so chunk seams and worker threads agree.
`WorldSettings.caveStructures` switches the whole integrated system off cleanly.

The three **legacy generators that ignored the caves** are disabled for new worlds:
`Structs.dungeons()`, `Structs.mineshafts()` and `Stronghold.apply()`. Surface villages now reject
a site that contains a cave mouth (`Structs.caveMouthNear`, used by both `planVillage` and
`Village314.plan`), so a foundation never hangs over an opening.

## Measured (headless, seed 20261107)

96×96 land sample, `-600..-505` × `-408..-313`:

| Metric | V6.31 (swiss cheese) | V6.32 (shafts) | **V6.34** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Carved overall | 16.1% | 5.0% | **9.0%** |
| Carved in top 20 blocks | 19.3% | 1.2% | **4.4%** |
| Open surface columns | 930 (10%) | 40 (0.4%) | **121 of 9216 (1.3%)** |
| Single-block vertical shafts | many | the openings themselves | **1** |
| Openings continuing into the network | — | 40/40 | **84/121** |

The remaining 37 openings are shallow grazes where the volume just clips the surface — an
overhang rather than a shaft. Cross-sections in `docs/screenshots/v6.34-caves.png` show the old
rectangular shafts on top and the new angled mouths below. These are **software renders of the
generator, not GPU screenshots**; the look still needs an in-game playtest.

All six structure types were probed against a world with `caveStructures=false`: every type builds
hundreds of blocks (`built=415..1911` in the regression sample), and natural worm/cheese/hall air
appears inside the carved space of five of the six types. The tiny spherical geode interiors are
the rare case in any given sample — a second wider probe found 10.9% geode overlap there. Because
`air()` is the carver's own function, the intersection is structural, not a probability trick.

## Old saves

`WorldSettings` now clamps `cavever` to 1..7. `caveVer` 1–6 keep their own carver and their own
structure set, unchanged. Only **Create New World** with *Caves & Cliffs* enabled uses
`caveVer = 7`; the in-game trailer world was updated to match.
